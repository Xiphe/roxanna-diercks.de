# Roxanna Diercks

A bilingual website for grief companionship for people living between places.

## Language

**Companionship**: Roxanna's support through grief, loss, and transition, online or in person.

**Between Words**: The English collection of reflective writing and its accompanying newsletter.

**Interest list**: People requesting notice when the online pet grief group opens.

**Publication**: The complete set of pages, navigation, and media references published together.
